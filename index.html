The Story

Built in Boston.
Known worldwide.

Alex Diaz started breaking in Boston and never looked back. At eleven years old he won his first world title, a kid from Boston standing on top of the world. That was just the start.

He came up through the Floorlords Crew, the crew founded in Boston in 1981, and today he leads it as president. As a Red Bull BC One ambassador, he has battled, judged, and taught across 35+ countries, with 28+ years of teaching behind him.

From the cypher to the big screen to the Olympic stage, the mission stays the same: represent the culture right, and open the door for the next generation coming up behind him.
